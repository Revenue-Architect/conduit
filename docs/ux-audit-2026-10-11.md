# Conduit (Hermes) UX audit — 2026-10-11

Audited on the S25 (build 172, profile, dark mode, font scale 1.0) across every primary surface, plus a code pass over `lib/` (851 files). Screenshots are in [`ux-audit-2026-10-11/`](ux-audit-2026-10-11/). The yardstick is the app's own Hermez system and your approved KaizenOS V2 rules: sentence case, no wide-tracked uppercase, one accent, calm and physical.

## Verdict

The Hermes core is genuinely good. It has a strong editorial voice: big tight display type, the anime bot marks, the dark ground with one orange accent. The chat and run sheets are now clean.

What holds the app back is everything around that core. It reads as three apps stitched together:

- **Hermez**: the dark editorial screens (Home, bot page, Kanban, Scheduled agents, Spaces, sheets).
- **Upstream Conduit settings**: stock Material (centred thin titles, outlined floating-label fields, brown segmented controls).
- **An inverted light drawer**, with an orange rail.

On top of that, developer data leaks into almost every screen: raw IDs, cron strings, `State: scheduled`, a 19-line server dump, `BROWSER_EXEC`. And the things you actually came for are often below the controls: the task's answer, a schedule's health, your own chats among the automated runs.

## Keep

- Home's hierarchy: the "Hermes" display headline, the 2-column bot cards with portraits, and the "Since you were away" idea.
- The bot page: the portrait, the stat strip (385 chats · 74 skills · 11 schedules) and the "What default knows" section.
- The run control and sheet as they are now (single rail, folded output, instant run switching).
- Kanban's "Show 5 empty stages" fold, and the Spaces list (quiet and readable).
- The physical side-nav motion and the Hermez sheet morphs.

## Cross-cutting issues

These cause most of the screen-level problems below, so fixing them pays off many times over.

## Screen by screen

Severity: **P1** = hurts daily use or trust · **P2** = clear polish problem · **P3** = nice to have.

### Home ([01](ux-audit-2026-10-11/01-home.png))
- **P2 "Since you were away" says nothing.** It lists cron names with "ran" (teams-scout, proactive-judge, Commitment catcher). Show outcomes ("Triaged 4 emails · 1 needs you") or only failures and items needing attention. Hide it when everything simply ran fine.
- **P2** The "YOUR THINKING PARTNER" tagline: remove it (X1).
- **P3** The "BOT" eyebrow on every bot card is redundant. Descriptions truncate mid-phrase ("powered by …"), so use a short one-line role.
- **P3** There's no "continue where you left off" entry. The most-used action (back to the last chat) takes a trip through the drawer.

### Drawer ([02](ux-audit-2026-10-11/02-drawer.png))
- **P1 Conversations (X4).** Title-only rows are unscannable, and automated runs bury your own threads.
- **P2** Mixed containers: Kanban and Spaces are white cards, Scheduled agents sits outside a card with its icon misaligned, and Bots is a plain list. Use one row style.
- **P2** "Hermes Home: Bots, work, and what needs you" is a full card linking to the screen you usually came from. Make it a compact row, or the avatar row.
- **P3** "HA" avatar: it's unclear whose initials these are. Use your initial or the Hermes mark.

### Search ([03](ux-audit-2026-10-11/03-search.png))
- **P1 Results are hidden.** With a query typed, the drawer still shows Home, the 6 bots, Kanban and Spaces first. The results start about two screens down, so you see none while the keyboard is up.
- **P1 No match context.** The results for "shopify" were all email-triage titles, with no snippet showing where "shopify" matched.
- **P2** Search only covers conversations. Bots, Kanban tasks, Spaces pages and schedules can't be found.
- Fix: while the query isn't empty, replace the drawer body with grouped results (Chats, Tasks, Pages), each with a highlighted snippet, bot portrait and date.

### New chat and composer ([04](ux-audit-2026-10-11/04-new-chat.png), [05](ux-audit-2026-10-11/05-plus-panel.png))
- **P1 Which bot will answer is unclear.** The header pill says "Hermes Agent", while the thread later shows "default".
- **P2** The empty state is just "Start chatting". Show the bot's portrait and name, plus 2–4 starters drawn from its skills or recent chats.
- **P2** The "+" opens a keyboard-height panel holding a single "File" button. Make it an inline attach icon, or fill the panel (photo, camera, Space page, skill).
- **P3** The model chip shows "High" effort in the thread but not on a new chat. Show it the same way in both places.

### Chat thread ([06](ux-audit-2026-10-11/06-thread.png))
- **P2** Copy, speak, regenerate and ⋯ sit at full contrast under every answer. Fade them to muted until the answer is the latest or is pressed.
- **P2** The scroll-to-bottom button and the model chip float over the answer text mid-line. Give the bottom chrome a frosted band, as the top already has, or offset them.
- **P3** Raw URLs such as `http://100.88.112.22:8300/v1/sessions/debug` print in full. Render them as a titled link chip.

### Run sheet and tool inspector ([07](ux-audit-2026-10-11/07-inspector.png))
- Fixed today: the timeline, zero stats, repeated conversation, run switching, and run control placement.
- **P2** The result shows the prompt-injection wrapper `<untrusted_tool_result source="browser_exec">…`. Strip the wrapper for display.
- **P3** Runs 1/2/3 are bare numbers. Label each with what it did (the URL or a short verb), so you don't have to open all three.
- **P3** The eyebrows "CONVERSATION ACTIVITY", "DONE · BROWSER_EXEC", "INPUT" and "RESULT" fall under X1.

### Kanban board ([08](ux-audit-2026-10-11/08-kanban.png))
- **P1 Stage order is backwards.** Done is shown first, then Triage, then Ready. Use workflow order (Triage → Todo → Ready → … → Done), with Done collapsed at the bottom.
- **P2** The "+" circles hang off the right edge of each stage card.
- **P2** Each task shows two status marks: a green check and a green dot.
- **P3** The "H." header mark appears only on this page. Use the same header as Spaces and Scheduled agents.

### Kanban task ([09](ux-audit-2026-10-11/09-task-top.png), [10](ux-audit-2026-10-11/10-task-bottom.png))
- **P1 The answer is last.** The thing a Done task exists for ("Key answer: no B2B…") is at the very bottom, clamped to 2 lines. Put it straight under the title.
- **P2 Every control appears twice.** The chips (Done / kai / Priority 0) and the Actions buttons (Edit title / Assignee / Priority) do the same jobs. Make the chips tappable and delete the Actions card.
- **P2** "Move task" is 7 equal buttons, including the current status and Archive. Use one status pill that opens a menu, with Archive in ⋯.
- **P2** A full-width orange "Add comment" is the primary action on a finished task. Demote it.
- **P3** The raw ID as eyebrow, "Priority 0", "1 runs", and a nested "FILES / 1" fold inside a card.

### Scheduled agents ([11](ux-audit-2026-10-11/11-scheduled.png))
- **P1 No overview.** 15 jobs at about 650px each. Use one row per job: name, cadence in words, a last-result dot, and next run. Pin failed or overdue jobs to the top. Tap a row for detail.
- **P1 Delete is one tap away.** A red trash icon sits next to edit on every card. Move it into the detail view, with confirm or undo.
- **P2** Raw `State:` / `Delivery:` lines, cron strings and the orphan "ok" (X3).
- **P3** "Run history" is an orange link while "Run now" is plain white text. Pick one style.

### Spaces ([16](ux-audit-2026-10-11/16-spaces.png))
- **P3** Mixed date formats ("Oct 4, 11:18 AM" vs "6d ago"). The subtitle differs from the drawer's wording. Otherwise this is the calmest screen in the app.

### Settings: You and Hermes Agent ([12](ux-audit-2026-10-11/12-hermes-settings.png), [13](ux-audit-2026-10-11/13-settings-mid.png), [14](ux-audit-2026-10-11/14-server-status.png))
- **P1 Toolsets renders as a stray orange squiggle** in an empty card. That's a broken empty or loading state.
- **P1 "Reload skills · No installed skills"** contradicts the bot page's "74 Skills".
- **P2 Server status is a raw dump.** Show one line ("Healthy · v0.21.5 · updated 2m ago") with the rest under "Details".
- **P2** "Sign out of Hermes" sits in the same group as Reload skills and MCP servers, with the same orange tile. Separate it and colour it as destructive.
- **P2** Two save buttons ("Save viewer", "Save") mid-page. The Server URL field shows an orange border at rest, which reads as an error.
- **P3** Toggles have a dark thumb on an orange track, so on and off read alike. Stock Material styling overall (X6).

### Model picker ([15](ux-audit-2026-10-11/15-model-picker.png))
- **P2** The current model (gpt-6-luna) isn't visible in the list, which opens on OpenRouter Claude models. Put the current model and recents first.
- **P3** Raw model IDs with an unexplained "Fast available". Add a short hint (speed, cost or context).

## Plan

### Now: small changes with large payoff
1. **Labels (X1, X2)**: one change to `HermezType.technical` plus removing about 15 `toUpperCase()` calls. Every eyebrow in the app goes sentence case at a legible size.
2. **Search**: show results in place of the drawer body, with snippets.
3. **Kanban**: workflow stage order, the overflowing "+", the double status mark, answer first in the task sheet, and chips doubling as the editors.
4. **Scheduled agents**: compact rows, words instead of state and cron, delete moved into detail.
5. **Settings**: the Toolsets empty state, the skills count, the server status summary, a separate Sign out.
6. **Inspector**: strip the `<untrusted_tool_result>` wrapper for display.
7. **Naming**: the composer placeholder and new-chat header use the bot's name and portrait.

### Next
8. Separate Chats from Automated runs, with a bot portrait and time on each row.
9. "Since you were away" shows outcomes and attention items, not "ran".
10. A real "+" panel (or an inline attach icon), and starter prompts on new chats.
11. Move the settings screens onto Hermez components.
12. Model picker: current and recent models first, with friendly hints.

### Later
13. A real typeface (see Decisions).
14. Remove the upstream Conduit leftovers from your build.

## Decisions for you

1. **Typeface.** Everything renders in system Roboto. KaizenOS V2 uses Hanken Grotesk with tight headings. Should Hermes adopt it, so the two products share a voice?
2. **The orange diagonal slash.** Keep it as a signature on Home only, or remove it?
3. **The inverted light drawer.** It's deliberate (`drawer_shell_page.dart:111`), but at night it flashes a full white panel and brings in a third look. Keep it inverted, or match the app's theme?
4. **Automated runs.** Hide them from Conversations entirely (reachable from Scheduled agents), or show them as a collapsed group?

## Not covered

- Light mode, tablet layout, sign-in and onboarding, Notes, the terminal, workspace editors, and screen-reader passes.
- Colour contrast wasn't measured instrumentally, so judgements about muted text are visual only.
